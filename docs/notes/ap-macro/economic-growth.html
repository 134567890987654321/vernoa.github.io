Chapter 17 · Economic Growth & the Aggregate Production Function

Growth occurs when the production possibilities frontier shifts outward—more resources, better technology, or both.

Growth Indicators

  • PPF shift right: Economy can produce more of every good.
  • Real GDP growth rate: ((RGDPt − RGDPt−1) / RGDPt−1) × 100.
  • Productivity: Output per worker; the key long-run driver of living standards.

Drivers of Growth

  • Increased labor (population, participation, immigration)
  • More physical capital (machines, infrastructure)
  • Human capital accumulation
  • Technological progress and better institutions

Aggregate Production Function (APF)

Y = A × F(L, K, N)
  • Y: Real output
  • A: Total factor productivity (technology)
  • L: Labor input
  • K: Physical capital
  • N: Natural resources

Holding technology constant, diminishing returns set in as you add more of one input. Technological improvements shift the APF upward.
