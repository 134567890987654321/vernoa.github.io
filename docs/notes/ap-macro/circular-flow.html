Chapter 4 · Circular Flow & Markets

Every macro model rests on a simple loop: households own resources, firms hire those resources to make goods, and the resulting income fuels spending.

Four Players, Two Markets

Households

Own all factors of production and supply labor, land, and capital to the factor market.

Firms

Demand factors to produce goods and services, then sell output in product markets.

Goods & Services Market

Where households spend income on consumption and firms earn revenue.

Factor (Resource) Market

Where firms pay wages, rent, interest, and profit to households for resource use.

Real vs Monetary Flows

  • Real flows: Labor, land, capital move from households to firms; finished goods move back to households.
  • Monetary flows: Income (wages, rent, interest, profits) flows from firms to households; consumption spending and investment flow to firms.
  • Balanced loop: In equilibrium, total spending equals total income equals total output.

Factor Payments

  • Labor → Wages
  • Land → Rent
  • Capital → Interest
  • Entrepreneurship → Profit

These payments add up to national income.

Adding Government & Financial Markets

More complete models insert leakages and injections:

  • Taxes: Leak income from households and firms to the government.
  • Transfers & Gov Spending: Inject purchasing power back into the loop.
  • Savings & Investment: Flow through financial intermediaries connecting savers and borrowers.
