Chapter 10 · Financial System Basics

The financial system connects savers and borrowers. Understanding the instruments and institutions sets up later chapters on money and policy.

Savers, Borrowers & Intermediaries

  • Savers: Households, firms, and foreign investors with excess funds.
  • Borrowers: Businesses financing capital, governments covering deficits, households buying big-ticket items.
  • Financial intermediaries: Banks, mutual funds, pension funds channel saving into investment.

Bond Fundamentals

Definition

A bond is an IOU: the borrower promises to repay the principal plus fixed interest.

Key Variables

  • Face value (principal)
  • Coupon payment
  • Maturity date

Price ↔ Yield

Inverse relationship. When interest rates rise, existing bond prices fall, and vice versa.

Efficient Market Theory

  • Core idea: Asset prices reflect all available information.
  • Implication: Changes in stock prices follow a random walk; consistently "beating the market" is extremely difficult.
  • AP takeaway: Treat big stock price swings as demand/supply shocks, not as predictable events.
