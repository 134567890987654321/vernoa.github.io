Chapter 8 · Unemployment, Natural Rate & Okun's Law

Track who counts in the labor force, label the type of unemployment, and connect joblessness to lost output.

Labor Force Basics

  • Labor Force: People 16+ who are working or actively seeking work.
  • LF Participation Rate (LFPR): (Labor force / Adult population) × 100.
  • Unemployment Rate: (Unemployed / Labor force) × 100.
  • Discouraged workers: Have given up job search → not counted, so official unemployment understates weakness.

Types of Unemployment

Frictional

Between jobs or entering workforce. Short-term and inevitable.

Structural

Skills mismatch as technology or consumer preferences shift.

Seasonal

Predictable swings in certain industries (tourism, agriculture).

Cyclical

Caused by business cycle downturns. Only type policymakers try to eliminate.

Full Employment & Natural Rate

  • Full employment: Only frictional + structural + seasonal unemployment remain.
  • Natural Rate (NRU): The unemployment rate consistent with potential output.
  • Actual unemployment: NRU + cyclical unemployment.

Okun's Law

Each 1 percentage point that actual unemployment exceeds the natural rate creates roughly a 2% negative GDP gap (forgone output).

Use this rule to estimate the size of recessionary gaps on FRQs.
